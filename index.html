📚 WELCOME TO THE READING ROOM

Introduction

Every book tells a story. Together, books tell us something about the world we read in.

Books offer a window into changing cultural interests, reading preferences, and the global publishing industry. By examining book-related data, we can explore which genres appear across publication years, how ratings are distributed among genres and publishers, and where bestselling authors come from.

This dashboard brings together three visualisations to investigate these questions. Each offers a different perspective on books and reading, helping us discover patterns, compare distributions, and explore the diversity of the literary world.

1. How Do Reading Preferences Change Over Time?

Explore how the representation of different book genres changes across publication years.

Understanding Genre Trends

Reading preferences evolve alongside cultural interests, social changes, and developments in popular media.

What to Look For

Compare the relative representation of genres over time. Look for genres that grow, decline, or remain prominent across publication years.

2. Which Genres and Publishers Attract the Most Ratings?

Examine how total book ratings are distributed across categories and their publishers.

Genre and Publisher Distribution

Different categories contain books from a variety of publishers. A treemap represents this hierarchy using nested rectangles.

How to Read the Treemap

Larger rectangles represent higher summed ratings counts. Publishers are grouped within book categories, while less common publishers can be combined into an "Other" group.

3. Where Do Bestselling Authors Come From?

Explore the geographical distribution of bestseller entries according to authors' nationalities.

Global Author Representation

Authors from different countries contribute to the international book market. Comparing their representation can reveal which nationalities appear most frequently in the bestseller dataset.

What to Look For

Compare countries with higher and lower numbers of bestseller entries. Remember that the map reflects the records in this dataset, not the total number of authors or books worldwide.
