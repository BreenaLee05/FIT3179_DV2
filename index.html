Books & Reading: Global Perspectives

Exploring reading trends, genre popularity over time, and global author diversity.

What Genres Did People Favor Over Time?

Reading preferences continuously evolve across decades. By analyzing popular book releases across publication years, we can observe shifting cultural interests and literary movements.

From classic historical fiction to modern fantasy and sci-fi surges, these shifts demonstrate how societal events and media adaptations influence what readers consume.

Where Do Bestselling Authors Come From?

Author diversity plays a major role in global reading habits. This map visualizes the geographic distribution of bestselling authors by nationality.

While certain regions dominate high-volume bestseller entries, exploring global author presence reveals emerging international voices and highlighting differences in translated literary reach.
